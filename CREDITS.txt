Barrett M107A1 for Geronimo - credits
=====================================

3D model and textures
  Barrett M107A1 (with its 10-round magazine) from the WTT-Armory mod for SPT (Single Player Tarkov),
  with parts from Escape from Tarkov by Battlestate Games. Exported by the user from their own install,
  for personal use only. This plugin is not for distribution: the art ships only inside Barrett.dll,
  encrypted, and must not be shared.
